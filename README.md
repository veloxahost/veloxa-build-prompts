## How to write a good build prompt

1. **Name it.** A real-sounding business name gives the AI a voice and makes copy less generic.
2. **List the sections or pages** in the order you want them.
3. **Say what every form collects** — field by field.
4. **Give a look**: 2–3 colours, a mood (warm, minimal, bold), and "mobile first".
5. **Say where data lives** for apps: "save in the browser" keeps it simple.
6. **Iterate in small steps** after the first draft: one change per message beats one giant prompt.
